- 👥 **HR & attendance** — people and time tracking
- 💰 **Payroll** — accurate, auditable pay workflows
- 📦 **Inventory** — stock management across locations
- 🔄 **Offline-first sync** — keeping data consistent when the network isn't

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Engineering

> *"AI is my co-pilot, not my replacement."* — R2 handles the astromech work; the pilot still flies the ship.
